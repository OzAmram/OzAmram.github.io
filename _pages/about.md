## I'm Oz --- an AI safety researcher with a background in particle physics

I am currently a fellow at **[CBAI](https://www.cbai.ai/)**, working with Hadas Orgad. 

Right now my research interests revolve around **personas, subliminal learning, and mechanistic interpretability**. 
You can read about some of my projects on my [AI safety page](/ai-safety/).

In my former physics life, I was a Wilson Fellow at [Fermilab](https://www.fnal.gov/), a tenure-track position equivalent to assistant professor, working on the CMS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider. 
I joined Fermilab as a postdoc after completing my PhD in physics at Johns Hopkins University in 2022, and started the fellowship in 2026.

I specialized in applying ML methods to analyze complex particle physics data, with the hope of answering deep questions about the fundamental nature of the universe.
I was particularly excited about anomaly detection and using it to find unexpected new phenomena hiding in massive collider datasets. 
See my [physics projects](/projects/) for more. 

Check out my [LessWrong profile](https://www.lesswrong.com/users/oz-amram) for my recent AI safety work. 
I also have a [blog on Substack](https://ozamram.substack.com/).
